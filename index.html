01 The Alpha code

Not a gym you visit. A team you belong to. Technique first, discipline every session, competition when you're ready — and people behind you every step of the way.

  1. 01

    Training

    Structured sessions, six days a week.

  2. 02

    Discipline

    Respect, punctuality, consistency. On the mat and off it.

  3. 03

    Technique

    Fundamentals, repeated until they become instinct.

  4. 04

    Competition

    Championship preparation, with additional sessions in competition season.

  5. 05

    Growth

    Athletic development you can see, session after session.

  6. 06

    Community

    Trips, outings, football — a team beyond training.
